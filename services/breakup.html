WHY IT HAPPENED

The Real Reasons Behind Your Breakup

Beyond surface arguments and misunderstandings, Guru Ji helps identify the deeper relationship patterns and circumstances contributing to your separation.

Emotional Compatibility & Conflict

Differences in communication, expectations, attachment, and emotional needs can create cycles of intense connection followed by distance or conflict.

Outside Influence & Relationship Stress

Family pressure, friends, social expectations, or other outside circumstances can create doubt and tension between two people.

Third-Party Influence

Someone in your ex's life is actively pulling them away — filling their mind with doubt and redirecting their emotional energy toward someone or something else.

Family & Social Pressure

Parents, friends, or cultural pressures convinced your ex to end things. Their own desire for the relationship still exists — it has been suppressed by external voices.

Confusion & Emotional Uncertainty

Stress, uncertainty, unresolved conflict, and changing priorities can make someone question a relationship or pull away emotionally.

Communication Breakdown

Poor communication can cause small misunderstandings to become major conflicts, making it difficult for two people to understand each other's needs and intentions.
