THE REAL CAUSES

What Drives Couples Toward Divorce

Beyond surface arguments, relationship patterns, communication problems, family pressure, and emotional challenges can all contribute to serious marital difficulties.

Communication & Compatibility

Differences in communication, expectations, emotional needs, and long-term priorities can create repeated conflict and distance between spouses.

Outside Influence & Family Pressure

Family pressure, social expectations, outside opinions, or interference from others can create additional tension and disagreement within a marriage.

Third Party Involvement

A new relationship or third-party involvement can create emotional distance, uncertainty, and serious challenges within a marriage.

Emotional Stress & Uncertainty

Stress, uncertainty, unresolved disagreements, and changing priorities can cause one or both partners to question the future of their marriage.

Parental / Family Pressure

Pressure from parents, in-laws, or other family members can influence decisions and increase conflict between spouses.

Emotional Distance

When affection, intimacy, appreciation, and emotional connection decline over time, a marriage can begin to feel distant and disconnected.
